**Goal:** beat BoxTeacher on test mask AP50 (0.664; box AP50 0.703) with a better detector. The LACE masker `lace4px_simple` and its s′ ranking stay unchanged.

**Evidence behind it (val):**
- Masks fail on about 25% of correct boxes at box IoU 0.5–0.6, so box quality at IoU ≈ 0.6 is what drives mask AP50 (`results/diag_maskbox_val.json`).
- Recall on crowns under 24 px (26% of crowns) is 0.58, against BoxTeacher's 0.68.
- About 16% more top-ranked false positives than BoxTeacher.
- ConvNeXt-L was dropped: the DINOv3 paper (Tables 14–15) puts it 7.1 mIoU below ViT-L on frozen ADE20k, and our frozen run agreed (0.333 vs 0.44–0.47 at epoch 5).

**Dropped 2026-11-02 (Tom): second stage / refinement / learned mask-IoU scoring.** It doubles up with a better detector. It may be revisited later.

## Design
- **Backbone:** frozen DINOv3 ViT-L, layer 24.
  - 4 px features: 16 shifted passes, the masker's 128-d projection (`feat16p.py`, unchanged).
  - Optionally the 1024-d 8 px features (`feat_4p_*`).
- **Pixel branch (arm):** a small trainable CNN on the raw RGB tile (stride 2 → 4 → 8, 64–128 channels), fused into P3 by a zero-initialised projection.
- **Head:** FCOS pyramid (pre-registration 2), extended with a P2 level (stride 4) from the 4 px features. Level ranges as AdelaiDet, with P2 added for the smallest boxes (exact range fixed in the pre-registration).
- **Canopy:**
  - Training: cells > 50% canopy are excluded from the negatives at every level, P2 included.
  - Early stopping: crowd-arm box AP at IoU 0.6 (canopy ignored, as at test; replaces the `dapt` metric, which counts canopy as FP).
  - Selection: full-pipeline crowd-arm mask AP50.
- **Augmentation:** flips (exact on the RGB branch, approximate on cached features).

|---|---|---|
| A | 8 px (1024-d) + 4 px P2 (128-d) | no |

| C | A + pixel branch | yes |
| D | B + pixel branch | yes |

Reference: the existing pyramid head `fpn_s0` (8 px only), val mask AP50 0.691, test 0.648.

**Selection rule:** the highest val mask AP50 over arm × NMS {0.5, 0.6} × ranking {s′, detector s}. Any configuration within 0.005 of the best that is simpler wins, preferring B over A, no pixel branch over pixel branch, NMS 0.5, and detector s.
