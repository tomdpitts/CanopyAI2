## Evidence notes

**#1 SAM placement (the main new finding).**
- Released chain (SAM3-FT presets, `fast.yaml:1-21`): tilerizer -> detector -> aggregator(boxes) -> segmenter -> aggregator(masks). The segmenter builds `DetectionLabeledRasterCocoDataset` over `data_state.tiles_path` (the tilerizer's tiles) plus the aggregator's COCO (`segmenter.py:137-147`); the aggregator COCO is written grouped by `Col.TILE_PATH` (`pipeline.py:505`), i.e. each surviving box is prompted on the tile that produced it. In the benchmark (`engine/benchmark/segmenter/benchmark.py:79-96`) the "tiles" are the pre-cut 1024 px OAM-TCD test tiles.
- `target_tile_size` exists only as `getattr(config, "target_tile_size", 1777)` (`sam3.py:54`); `SegmenterConfig` has no such field (`engine/config_parsers/segmenter.py:5-29`), so 1777 is always used, applied to whatever tile the segmenter receives. "Default 1777" is verified; "the tile" is their subtile, not the 2048 image.
- SAM 3 encoder input is 1008^2 regardless (`facebook/sam3` `processor_config.json`: `size 1008`, `mask_size 288`). So the 1777 resize is irrelevant to encoder resolution; what matters is the field of view: ours 204.8 m, released benchmark 102.4 m, fast 124.4 m, quality 80.0 m.
- SAM FT training: SelvaMask at 0.045 m/px (`preprocessed_datasets.py:679`), crops 666-2666 px resized into [1024,1777] (`sam3_multi_selvamask_FT.yaml:60-69`) -> footprint 30-120 m. Our 204.8 m is outside the training range; released 102.4 m is inside.
- GT crown size (computed from `test_gt.json`, 25,705 crowns, sqrt bbox area @2048): p10/p25/p50/p75 = 15.3/23.3/42.0/69.5 px. At our encoder scale the median crown is 20.7 px / 5.9 logit cells; released per-subtile: 41.3 px / 11.8 cells.
- Consistent with the row's profile: best box AP50 (0.732) but box->mask loss 0.16 AP50 and mask AP75 0.086 (`scored_selvabox_bench_r2048_md600.json`).
- Same path in tab:gtbox FT arm: `gtbox_modal.py:45` ("the resolution CanopyRS fine-tuned and deploys at"), `:324` whole-tile resize to 1777. That row (0.623 mean IoU) is affected identically.
- Fix (GPU, SAM only; detector raw_dets are on the volume): prompt each surviving box on its source 1024 subtile resized to 1777, paste mask into the 2048 frame. Do it together with the seam fix: with the interior-edge cull every survivor lies >=51.2 px inside its source subtile, so per-subtile SAM cannot truncate it. Source subtile is recoverable by matching `boxes_2048` to `raw_dets[*].boxes_2048` (both rounded to 2 dp, `:214,247`).

## Released edge-band rule

Code: geodataset v0.6.5 `geodataset/aggregator/aggregator.py`.
- Default `edge_band_buffer_percentage=0.05` (`:43`; CanopyRS `engine/config_parsers/aggregator.py:16`; set explicitly 0.05 in both aggregators of both SAM3-FT presets, `fast.yaml:12,19`, `quality.yaml:12,19`). Passed through at CanopyRS `engine/components/aggregator.py:177`.
- Order in `Aggregator.__init__` (`:68-81`): `_validate_polygons` -> **`_filter_edge_polygons`** (if pct > 0, `:71-72`) -> write pre-aggregated gpkg -> `_prepare_scores` -> `_remove_low_score_polygons` -> `_apply_nms_algorithm`. So the cull is **before** the score threshold and **before** NMS, on every raw per-tile polygon.
- Semantics (`:754-786`): tile extent = full bounds of the tile GeoTIFF (`:646-656`). `safe = tile.buffer(-(pct * tile_width))` (`:767-769`), width from `geom.bounds[2]-bounds[0]` in CRS units. Polygon kept iff `geometry.within(safe)` for **its own** `tile_id` (`:774-783`). All four edges, symmetric inset. Empty/missing safe geom -> keep (`:777-778`).
- For 1024 px tiles: margin 51.2 px; safe window [51.2, 972.8] in subtile coords. On our clamped grid the safe x-intervals are [51.2,972.8], [563.2,1484.8], [1075.2,1996.8]: consecutive overlap 409.6 px.
- Agg-1 polygons are detector **boxes**; agg-2 polygons are SAM **masks** (same rule again).
- Outer frame in the released tilerizer: tiles are **not clamped** to the raster; origins `range(0, W, step)` (`tilerize/raster_tilerizer.py:162-163,189,236`), out-of-raster pixels read as 0 (`geodata/raster.py:409`, `boundless=True, fill_value=0`) and the tile keeps full `tile_size` bounds (`raster.py:109-118`). So the top/left frame always coincides with a tile edge and is culled; the right/bottom frame is interior to a padded tile wherever such a tile is generated. With the default skip threshold 0.75 (`config_parsers/tilerizer.py:15`) the rolling-buffer loop breaks once `height` is read (`raster_tilerizer.py:206-215`): at 2048 px / 1024 / step 512 this yields rows {0,512,1024} x cols {0,512,1024,1536}, i.e. right frame recovered, bottom frame culled; at the fast preset (2926 px raster, 1777, step 888) rows and cols both reach a padded tile, so only top/left are culled. Paper.tex:1211 ("no neighbouring subtile can recover them", 16.0% recall cap) therefore overstates the released behaviour on the outer frame; moot once the cull is applied at interior edges only.
- The OAM-TCD tile-level benchmark never runs the aggregator (above), so no edge band there; its GT is cut per subtile with a >=40% mask-area rule (`oam_tcd.py:597-602`), making truncated predictions consistent with truncated GT in that protocol.
