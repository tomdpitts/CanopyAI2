Scope: the Box2Mask-T rows (R-50, Swin-L). The stitcher seam/dedup is excluded because it is audited separately. Read-only: no Modal job was launched, and no existing file was modified.

## Evidence notes

- **LR never decayed.** `EV/r50_20260901_153210.log.json`: every train record has `lr` 1e-05, iters 50→20050, and 12 val records (0.414 … 0.479 … 0.445). In `box_supervised_baselines/train_box2mask_swin-l_s0_valcurve_20260902_051355.log.json` every lr is 1e-05, iters 50→30100, with 9 vals. In `..._104024.log.json` every lr is 1e-05, iters 30150→32400, with 1 val at record index 1, before the first train record. P:286-290 already concedes that the R-50 run "never reached" its decay.
- **The duplicate eval.** Phase-1 eval 9 has bbox_mAP 0.314 and AP50 0.590, and so does the resumed run's single eval. Predict's candidate list (`predict_box2mask_swin-l_s0_test.json`) shows `iter_30114` at 0.59, the same weights. The next new evaluation would have been at iter 33460, and the log stops at 32400.
- **Swin-L stopping.** `train_box2mask_swin-l_s0.json` sched shows `max_hours` 5.0 and patience 4. The val sequence after the peak is 0.590 and 0.590, i.e. 2 non-improving evals, so `APEarlyStop` could not have fired. P:372 records "killed at epoch 4.8".
- **Output cap.** From the raw subtile JSONs in `/Users/tompitts/dphil/feat_cache/b2m_raw/{r50,swinl}/` (3951 files each): R-50 has 269,452 instances, median 92 per subtile, 108 subtiles at 100. Swin-L has 274,598, median 94, 1137 at 100. In capped subtiles the median score of the 100th slot is 0.25 for both backbones.
- **Released Swin-L.** B-SwL contains only `_base_ = ['./box2mask_swin-t-p4-w7-224_lsj_8x2_50e_coco.py']` plus backbone and query overrides. B-SwT has `samples_per_gpu=4`, `lr=0.0002`, `step=[163890, 177546]`, `max_iters = 184376`, with `custom_keys` built from `depths=[2,2,6,2]`.
- **Paper numbers match the files.** The r2048 scores in T:642-643 (0.563/0.231/0.602 and 0.433/0.157/0.497) match `NATIVE_RASTER.md:284-286`. R-50 "stopped at epoch 6, selected epoch 4" checks out: 20076/3346 = 6.0 and 13384/3346 = 4.0.

## Remediation (for DEFECTs 1-3; nothing was run)

1. **Finish both schedules from the latest checkpoints so both decays apply.** R-50 runs `latest.pth`/`iter_20076` → 30114. Swin-L runs `iter_30114` → 40152. Disable `APEarlyStop` and `WallClockGuard`, keep the per-epoch or half-epoch evals, and then let predict choose the maximum `best_score` across the pre- and post-decay files (M:857-879 already handles several `best_*.pth`). Each completion is about 10k micro-iterations: roughly 2 h on an A100-40GB for R-50 and 2 h on an A100-80GB for Swin-L, using the `--resume` path with `--detach`. The resume will re-evaluate the start checkpoint once more (a harmless duplicate).
2. If (1) is not run, correct T:654 to: "two later evaluations below the peak; training was stopped by a compute budget before its scheduled LR decay, so neither Box2Mask row has had its LR annealed". Also correct P:369.
3. Add the following to `tab:geometry` (T:1191): test scale (1333,800)→(1024,1024); canopy blackout of the training imagery; the rescaled schedule with early or budget stop and no LR decay; Swin-L lr 1e-4 against the inherited 2e-4. Separately, correct "1.2%" at T:655 to 1.0% (39/3951, centre-in-subtile definition), or record the definition behind 46.
